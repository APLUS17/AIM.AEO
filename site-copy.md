## 7. Simple FAQ

* **Headline:**
  ## Frequently asked questions.

#### How is this different from normal SEO?
Normal SEO helps you rank on Google so people can click your link. AEO makes sure that when someone asks ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity "what's the best tool for X?", the AI names your product directly.

#### How fast do AI answers update?
Tools that search the live web (like Perplexity and SearchGPT) usually update within 1 to 2 weeks once they crawl your updated site. Other models update as they refresh their data, which our setup helps speed up.

#### Can't I just use a tracker like Peec AI myself?
Trackers only show you a chart of how often you get mentioned. They don't fix your code, update your data, or fix what the AI says about you. We do the hands-on work to improve your numbers.
