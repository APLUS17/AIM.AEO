What's in your report.

Five things add up to your score out of 100.

Score breakdown100 pts

01 Up to 40 pts

Sentiment

The biggest piece of your score: is AI saying good things, bad things, or nothing? We check three layers:

  • Overall tone: positive, negative, or neutral.
  • Tone by topic: how it shifts across support, pricing, or product quality.
  • Source trust: how reliable those sources are.

Good sign: "known for great onboarding." Red flag: AI repeating old complaints or outdated reviews.

02 Up to 20 pts

Presence quality

Does AI actually know you, or just your name?

  • Depth: how much real detail AI gives.
  • Source authority: how credible its sources are.
  • Data richness: features, pricing, and use cases covered.
03 Up to 20 pts

Brand recognition

How well AI knows you. A high score means real specifics, not just confirming you exist.

04 Up to 10 pts

Share of voice

Where you rank against competitors in your category: your estimated share of mentions, plus a bonus for ranking near the top.

Want it tracked weekly? That's what we do.

05 Up to 10 pts

Market position

How AI places you in your market. We check two things:

  • Category role: leader, challenger, or niche player.
  • Reputation: innovator, disruptor, or traditional.

A low score means AI gets mixed or thin signals about your position. Now you know what to fix.
