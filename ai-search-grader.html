What's in your report.

We grade your brand on five things. Each one adds up to your total score out of 100.

Score breakdown100 pts
Sentiment · 40 Presence quality · 20 Brand recognition · 20 Share of voice · 10 Market position · 10
01 Up to 40 pts

Sentiment

The biggest piece of your score. Is the AI saying good things about you, bad things, or nothing much at all? We look at three layers:

  • Overall tone: whether AI describes you positively, negatively, or neutrally.
  • Tone by topic: how that shifts across areas like support, pricing, or product quality.
  • Source trust: how reliable the sources behind those opinions are.

Good sign: "known for great onboarding." Red flag: AI repeating old complaints or outdated reviews.

02 Up to 20 pts

Presence quality

Does AI actually know you, or just your name?

  • Depth: how much real detail AI gives about you.
  • Source authority: how credible the sources that shaped that view are.
  • Data richness: how complete the picture is (features, pricing, use cases).
03 Up to 20 pts

Brand recognition

How well-known you are to the AI. A high score means it can talk about you with real specifics, not just confirm you exist.

04 Up to 10 pts

Share of voice

Where you rank against competitors when AI answers questions in your category. You get your estimated share of mentions, plus a bonus for ranking near the top.

Want this tracked every week instead of once? That's what we do.

05 Up to 10 pts

Market position

How AI places you in your market. We check two things:

  • Category role: leader, challenger, or niche player.
  • Reputation: innovator, disruptor, or traditional.

A low score usually means AI is getting mixed or thin signals about where you stand. Now you'll know which ones to fix.
