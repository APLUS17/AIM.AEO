## P01 · Mon 2026-10-26 · Comparison

**Headline:** AEO vs GEO vs SEO: which term to use, and does it matter?

**Subheadline:** The three labels overlap more than they differ. Here is what each one names, and which to use with your buyers.

**Pattern:** Comparison. Proof element: Three-term comparison table. Next step for the reader: free AI Search Grader.

**What it covers**

- What SEO, AEO and GEO each name, in a table.
- Where the term GEO came from (a 2023 research paper).
- Why the label matters less than the target: being named in the answer.

**What you will learn:** AEO, GEO and SEO are mostly one job under different names. The real shift is that fewer people click through when an AI answer is on the page.

**Data and stats I found (checked at the original publisher)**

- Google: "There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary." Also: "You don't need to create new machine readable files, AI text files, or markup", and "no special schema.org structured data that you need to add". SEO best practices remain relevant.
  - Source: [Google Search Central, AI features and your website (updated 2025-12-10)](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) (Primary). Retrieved 2026-10-18.
- Pew: with an AI summary, people clicked a regular result link in 8% of visits, against 15% with no summary. They clicked a link inside the summary in 1% of visits. 26% of pages with a summary ended the browsing session, against 16% without. 58% of respondents had at least one search with an AI summary; 18% of all searches produced one.
  - Source: [Pew Research Center short read, 2025-07-22](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) (Primary). Retrieved 2026-10-18.
  - Caveat: 900 US adults, 68,879 Google searches, March 2025. Observational. Google only. Panel of people who installed tracking software.
- The GEO paper (Aggarwal et al., first submitted 2023-11-16, accepted to KDD 2024): "GEO can boost visibility by up to 40% in generative engine responses."
  - Source: [arXiv 2311.09735 (abstract)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) (Primary). Retrieved 2026-10-18.
  - Caveat: "Up to" is an upper bound. The benefit varies by domain. "Visibility" is defined inside the paper's framework.
- SparkToro: 68.01% of US Google searches ended without a click in January to April 2026 (Similarweb desktop and mobile web panel), against 60.45% in 2024 (Datos panel). SparkToro says the rise is "almost certainly driven by the massive growth in AI Overviews".
  - Source: [SparkToro, published 2026-06-09 (updated 2026-06-18)](https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click/) (Primary). Retrieved 2026-10-18.
  - Caveat: The two years use different panels (SparkToro calls it "apples and oranges"). Excludes the Google mobile app. The data does not isolate AI Overviews as the cause.

_Used in the draft: google, pew_click, geo (spark is available but not used in the draft)._
