### Reddit Post 2: For `r/SEO` / `r/marketing`
**Title:** "AI doesn't rank content—it selects answers." The practical playbook for AEO in 2026

```text
Everyone in SEO has spent the last year panicking about Google AI Overviews and traffic drops.

The fundamental issue is that traditional SEO was built for a catalog model: you optimized for keyword frequency and backlink counts so Google would rank you among 10 blue links.

LLMs don't operate on a catalog model; they operate on a synthesis model. When a user asks a question, the retrieval engine (RAG) searches for:
1. Entity confidence: Is the business unambiguous in Wikidata / Schema / official registries?
2. Factual consensus: Do independent sources (reviews, Reddit threads, directories) corroborate the claims?
3. Extraction density: Can the LLM lift a 2-sentence direct answer without wading through 1,500 words of introductory fluff?

If you are still writing 2,500-word "Ultimate Guides" with zero structured data and no direct question-answering architecture, you are optimizing for an algorithm that is actively being phased out.

What are you guys seeing in terms of citation share on Perplexity and ChatGPT Search for your clients?
```
