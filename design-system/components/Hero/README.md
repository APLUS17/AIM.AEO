# Hero

The above-the-fold statement: a `display` headline, a `lead` paragraph in `color-steel`, a primary + ghost button pair, and a cluster of overlapping DataCards on the right.

**Consumer provides:** the headline (≤ 8 words), one lead sentence, two actions, and two or three DataCards with real figures.

- Sits directly on `surface-page-canvas` with `spacing-140` above it; one hero per page.
- Headline is PolySans 66px weight 400, line-height 0.91 — never bold, never looser than 1.25.
- The data cluster is the only imagery: no photos or illustrations.
- Below 720px the columns stack and the headline steps down to `heading-lg`.
